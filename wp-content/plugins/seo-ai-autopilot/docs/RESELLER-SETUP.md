# Connect SEO AI AutoPilot to Google Search Console

## What you need

A Google account that has access to your site in Google Search Console.

## Steps

1. In your WordPress admin, go to **SEO AI AutoPilot → Settings**
2. In the **Google Search Console** section, click **Connect with Google**
3. Sign in with your Google account when prompted
4. Click **Allow** to grant SEO AI AutoPilot read access to your Search Console data
5. You'll be returned to your WordPress admin with a green confirmation message

That's it. The plugin will start collecting data from Google Search Console automatically.

## What if I have multiple sites in Search Console?

After connecting, click **Load Properties** in the OAuth card. A dropdown will appear listing every property your Google account can access. Pick the one for this WordPress site and click **Save Settings**.

## How do I disconnect?

In the same Settings panel, click **Disconnect**. This removes the connection both locally and at Google's side.

## What does this app see?

Read-only access to:

- The list of sites you own in Google Search Console
- Search performance metrics (clicks, impressions, position, CTR) for the property you select
- Your email address (so we can show you which Google account is connected)

It cannot make changes to your Search Console settings, your Google account, or anything else.
